Emission Info:

What is Carbon Footprint?

Carbon footprint is the amount of greenhouse gases (mainly CO2) which are generated by human related activities such as:

  • Driving
  • Flying
  • Heating
  • Diet
  • General Industrial Actions

Individual people aren't the only ones with a carbon footprint as Organizations, Businesses and full countries can have a carbon footprint.

Why Does Tracking Your Carbon Footprint Matter?
Greenhouse gases act like a blanket around the Earth, trapping heat in the atmosphere and driving global climate change. Because modern systems rely heavily on fossil fuels, almost every choice we make—from the food we purchase to how we power our homes—has a hidden environmental cost. By measuring your footprint, you can identify the high-impact areas of your daily routine where simple changes can create the biggest reductions.

How to Reduce Your Footprint?

Transport:

  • Use Public Transport
  • Walk or Cycle when Possible
  • Switch to EV vehicles
  • Reduce your number of long-haul flights

Energy:

  • Improve the insulation in your home
  • Use LED lighting
  • Switch to efficient heating options
  • Install solar panels on your roof

Food:

  • Reduce food waste by planning meals, storing food properly and compost
  • Buy local food to minimize food miles
  • Switch from beef diets to chicken or more plant based diets

Lifestyle:

  • Buy second hand clothes
  • Fix damaged clothes where possible
  • Get the most out of your clothes

FAQ

What is COZero?

Answer: COZero is a carbon footprint tracker web application that lets you track your emissions, set goals, earn achievements and compete with friends.

Question: What metrics are used to find my estimated carbon footprint?

Answer: Our questionnaire uses four areas of our everyday life: Travel (how far you travel), Home Energy (your heating type and monthly usage), Food (diet) and Lifestyle (How often you buy new clothes and recycle).

Question: How do I earn points to climb the leaderboard?

Answer: By earning achievements, achievements have assigned emission points that you can get by getting the achievements.

Question: Can I see my friends position on the leaderboard?

Answer: Yes you can, the main leaderboard only shows you and the top tens points and position but there is a friend leaderboard that lets you see how you rank compare to friends.
